Please check out that in 06/2025 started project: [https://github.com/anjakuchenbecker/smartbox_smart_dashboard_aprilia_rs125_rs250](https://github.com/anjakuchenbecker/smartbox_smart_dashboard_aprilia_rs125_rs250).

In this repo I provide my concepts about my self build "Smartbox" / Smart Dashboard which replaces the old cockpit of my Aprilia RS125 two-stroke race track bike. It fits also to the Aprilia RS250 version and will be later also attached to it.

This project transforms the old cockpit into a modern Dashboard and performs detailed telemetry data logging with support of an Raspberry Pi 4B (8GB), a self build wiring harness and a bunch of electronic components.

Check out also our corresponding Vlog [https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLNJk0Ec1lpK8](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLNJk0Ec1lpK8).
